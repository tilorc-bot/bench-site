\(p \in \mathbb{Z},\quad p \text{ prime},\quad p > 2\)
Claim
\(\displaystyle \left(-1\right)^{p} = -1\)
Expected
true equal at every admissible point

the two sides are equal at every admissible point

Lean proof: included

(case demo-prime-01
  (category primes)
  (source claude-demo-2026-10 "first committed in 2f2f2cd")

  (var p integer)
  (assume (prime p))
  (assume (> p 2))
  (ask (= (pow -1 p) -1))
  (expect true)
  (lean-proof "obtain ⟨q, hq, rfl⟩ := h0.2; simp at h1; simp [AE.pow, Complex.log_neg_one]; rw [Complex.exp_nat_mul, Complex.exp_pi_mul_I, (hq.odd_of_ne_two (by omega)).neg_one_pow]"))

demo-prime-02 Lean-verified

Given
\(p \in \mathbb{Z},\quad p \text{ prime}\)
Claim
\(\displaystyle \left(-1\right)^{p} \overset{?}{=} -1\)
Expected
contingent true at \(p = 3\); false at \(p = 2\)

equal at some admissible points, different at others

witness (true): \(p = 3\)

witness (false): \(p = 2\)

2 is the only even prime, so the equation is neither always true nor always false.

Lean proof: included

(case demo-prime-02
  (category primes)
  (source claude-demo-2026-10 "first committed in 2f2f2cd")

  (var p integer)
  (assume (prime p))
  (ask (= (pow -1 p) -1))
  (expect contingent)
  (witness true (p 3)
    (lean-proof "refine ⟨⟨3, by simp⟩, ⟨trivial, 3, Nat.prime_three, by simp⟩, by simp, ?_⟩; rw [AE.pow_eq_cpow (by norm_num), Complex.cpow_ofNat]; norm_num"))
  (witness false (p 2)
    (lean-proof "refine ⟨⟨2, by simp⟩, ⟨trivial, 2, Nat.prime_two, by simp⟩, by simp, ?_⟩; rw [AE.pow_eq_cpow (by norm_num), Complex.cpow_ofNat]; norm_num"))

  (note "2 is the only even prime, so the equation is neither always true nor always false."))
