Solved vs time

Per system routine: how many cases it solved against its time per solved case, on one suite or summed over all of them (a routine is in the sum only if it ran on every suite; its times are pooled). Official cases only, as on the suite pages.

time per solved case

y: cases solved (true and false cases answered correctly: the table's solved row). x: wall-clock time per solved case, its median or mean, log scale; only solved cases are timed (a timeout or no verdict is never a fast answer), so a system that solves only easy cases can look fast. Dotted: the Pareto frontier, the routines no other one beats on both (as fast or faster and solving as many or more). Hollow marks: routines with wrong answers; the site ranks any wrong answer below every wrong-free system, so the frontier leaves them out unless asked. A colour and a marker shape per group, its routines joined by a thin line.
