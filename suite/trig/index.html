Trigonometric identities (demo)

A small public demo suite: Pythagorean and double-angle identities, inverse functions composed with their functions (true only on the principal range), and periodicity. Official vocabulary only.

licence CC0-1.0

Categories
identities
Algebraic trigonometric identities over the reals or complexes, and where they fail (sqrt of a square).
inverse
asin(sin x) = x and friends: true exactly on the principal range of the inverse.
periodicity
Shifts by multiples of pi: periods, half-periods, and where a shifted value coincides.
