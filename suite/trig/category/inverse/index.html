Given
\(x \in \mathbb{R},\quad x > 0,\quad x < 3\)
Claim
\(\displaystyle \arccos\left(\cos x\right) = x\)
Expected
true equal at every admissible point

the two sides are equal at every admissible point

Lean proof: included

(case trig-acos-01
  (category inverse)
  (source claude-trig-2026-10 "first committed in cd62696")

  (var x real)
  (assume (positive x))
  (assume (< x 3))
  (ask (= (acos (cos x)) x))
  (expect true)
  (lean-proof "obtain ⟨r, rfl⟩ := AE.IsReal.exists hd0; simp at h0 h1; refine ⟨by simp, ?_⟩; rw [AE.cos_eq, ← Complex.ofReal_cos, AE.acos_ofReal (Real.neg_one_le_cos _) (Real.cos_le_one _), Real.arccos_cos (by linarith) (by linarith [Real.pi_gt_three])]"))

trig-asin-01 Lean-verified

Given
\(x \in \mathbb{R}\)
Claim
\(\displaystyle \arcsin\left(\sin x\right) \overset{?}{=} x\)
Expected
contingent true at \(x = 0\); false at \(x = 3\)

equal at some admissible points, different at others

witness (true): \(x = 0\)

witness (false): \(x = 3\)

Lean proof: included

(case trig-asin-01
  (category inverse)
  (source claude-trig-2026-10 "first committed in cd62696")

  (var x real)
  (ask (= (asin (sin x)) x))
  (expect contingent)
  (witness true (x 0) (lean-proof "norm_num [AE.asin, AE.sqrt]"))
  (witness false (x 3)
    (lean-proof "refine ⟨by simp, by simp, ?_⟩; rw [AE.sin_eq, ← Complex.ofReal_ofNat, ← Complex.ofReal_sin, AE.asin_ofReal (Real.neg_one_le_sin _) (Real.sin_le_one _)]; exact_mod_cast (by linarith [Real.arcsin_le_pi_div_two (Real.sin 3), Real.pi_lt_d2] : Real.arcsin (Real.sin 3) ≠ 3)")))
