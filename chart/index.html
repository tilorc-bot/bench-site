Solved vs time

Per system routine: how many cases it solved against its time per solved case, on one suite, as a weighted index over the suites (the settings under the selector), or summed over all of them (the raw sum: a routine is in it only if it ran on every suite; its times are pooled). Official cases only, as on the suite pages. Since 27 October 2026 the page opens on the weighted index; the raw sum it showed before is all suites, raw sum in the selector.

time per solved case
weight per case

Published defaults, version 1 (2026-10-24): weight per case simplipy 1; examples, trig 0; λ = 3; times relative to sympy-simplify. Only suites taken from an outside source weigh by default. Change them here; the link keeps your settings.

y: cases solved (true and false cases answered correctly: the table's solved row). x: wall-clock time per solved case, its median or mean, log scale; only solved cases are timed (a timeout or no verdict is never a fast answer), so a system that solves only easy cases can look fast. Dotted: the Pareto frontier, the routines no other one beats on both (as fast or faster and solving as many or more). Hollow marks: routines with wrong answers; the site ranks any wrong answer below every wrong-free system, so the frontier leaves them out unless asked. A colour and a marker shape per group, its routines joined by a thin line. Whiskers (median view): the 95% interval of the median over the timed cases (distribution-free, from the cases' order statistics): how far the median could move with other cases of the same kind and each one's timing noise; it does not cover a slower or faster run of the whole machine.

y: the weighted score: per suite the share of its cases solved, less λ times the share answered wrong, combined with the suites' weights (a suite's share of the total is its weight per case times its cases); 100% is every case solved. x: time per solved case (median or mean) divided by the reference routine's, per suite, and combined as a geometric mean weighted by the suites' shares (log scale; ×1 is the reference). A routine that solves no timed case on a suite is timed over the other suites. Dotted: the Pareto frontier over every routine (wrong answers are priced in by λ). Hollow marks: routines with wrong answers on a weighed suite.
